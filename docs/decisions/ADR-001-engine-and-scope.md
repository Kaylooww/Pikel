# ADR-001 — Godot, GDScript, local-first vertical slice

**Status:** accepted as initial project recommendation on 2026-11-16. **Context:** small AI-assisted team, self-made Blender models, cursor-targeted 3D sports gameplay, PC-first. **Decision:** Godot 4.7.2 (standard) and GDScript; isolate ball physics/rules from presentation; build local practice, rules-complete singles, then authoritative networking. **Rationale:** free and lightweight engine, simple project-text workflows for AI review, direct GLB integration, capable dedicated servers. **Tradeoffs:** smaller AAA middleware ecosystem than Unity/Unreal; disciplined networking design and test infrastructure necessary. **Triggers for review:** demonstrated engine bottleneck, platform contract or team expertise changes. Do not switch engine speculatively.
